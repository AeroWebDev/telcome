How it works

From routed message to settled revenue.

ButterflyIPRN operates as the connective layer between businesses, mobile operators and carrier partners — moving SMS and voice traffic across authorised international routes with full delivery visibility at every hop.

SMS traffic

IPRN SMS — A2P & P2P

IPRN SMS traffic is generated whenever applications, platforms, or individual users send messages across our premium international routes. We support two distinct traffic types, each with its own routing and settlement logic.

A2P · Application-to-Person

Enterprise-driven messaging

Businesses route time-sensitive, high-trust traffic through our network, including:

  • OTP & identity verification codes
  • Banking and transaction alerts
  • Marketing & promotional campaigns
  • Delivery and logistics notifications
  • Security and fraud-alert messaging

Every delivered message passes through authorised carrier routes, with revenue generated per the commercial agreement between operators, aggregators and traffic partners.

P2P · Person-to-Person

Cross-network messaging

End users exchange SMS internationally through our carrier network. Depending on route, destination and the underlying commercial agreement, this traffic can generate settlement revenue shared across participating partners — governed entirely by operator terms and regulatory alignment in each market.

Voice traffic

IPRN Voice — Wholesale Termination & Origination

IPRN Voice enables international calls to move through premium carrier networks with intelligent routing and consistently monitored call quality. This service tier is built for partners who need throughput and reliability at wholesale scale:

  • Telecom carriers
  • Wholesale voice providers
  • Call centres
  • Enterprise communication platforms
  • International telecom partners
The process

Five steps, every corridor.

  1. 01

    Traffic is initiated

    A business, application, or end user originates SMS or voice traffic bound for an international destination.

  2. 02

    Intelligent routing

    ButterflyIPRN selects the optimal path across our global carrier network — balancing latency, cost, and deliverability in real time.

  3. 03

    Carrier delivery

    The destination mobile operator completes delivery of the message or call through trusted, authorised carrier partners.

  4. 04

    Confirmation & monitoring

    Delivery reports (DLRs) confirm successful SMS completion; voice calls are continuously monitored for quality and route integrity.

  5. 05

    Settlement

    Revenue is settled between operators, aggregators, and authorised partners according to their commercial agreements.

Partner revenue model

What determines settlement value.

Partners generating legitimate SMS or voice traffic through ButterflyIPRN may earn revenue based on a combination of commercial and technical factors:

Traffic volume

Total qualifying messages or minutes delivered per corridor.

Destination & carrier agreements

Rates vary by country and the underlying operator settlement terms.

Route quality

Deliverability, latency, and completion rates across the route.

Compliance standing

Adherence to local regulation, operator policy, and industry rules.

Important notice

Revenue opportunities depend on traffic quality, destination markets, operator agreements, applicable regulations, and commercial settlement terms — earnings are not guaranteed and vary by market and partnership structure. All services operate under authorised carrier agreements and must comply with local laws, operator policies, and industry regulations.
