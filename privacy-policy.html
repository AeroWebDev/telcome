Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

At ButterflyIPRN ("we", "our", or "us"), protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality of your personal and technical data is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit sales inquiries, or connect to our global messaging and voice carrier network.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with ButterflyIPRN, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, work email address, phone number, company name, job title, billing details, and communication preferences provided through our contact forms or onboarding documentation.
  • Technical Network & Interconnect Data: IP addresses, SMPP bind credentials, API requests, routing logs, delivery receipt status (DLRs), signaling metadata, traffic timestamps, and origin/destination carrier codes necessary for routing messaging and voice traffic.
  • Website Usage Data: Automated server logs, browser type, operating system, referral URLs, time spent on pages, and standard cookie data to optimize website performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Provisioning, maintaining, and optimizing our A2P SMS, P2P SMS, IPRN, SMPP, and Wholesale Voice services;
  • Processing interconnect contracts, invoicing, traffic billing, and route reconciliation;
  • Detecting, preventing, and mitigating fraudulent messaging, spam, unauthorized network intrusion, and cyber threats;
  • Responding to technical support inquiries, SLA reporting, and customer service requests;
  • Complying with statutory telecommunications regulations, lawful interception mandates, and legal obligations.

3. Data Protection & Carrier Interconnection

ButterflyIPRN acts as a carrier-grade messaging conduit. Traffic content and transmission metadata processed on behalf of enterprise clients and operators are handled in strict compliance with applicable telecommunication laws and data protection standards (including GDPR and regional carrier mandates).

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or transmission logs to third-party marketers.
  • Data is shared only with downstream mobile network operators (MNOs), licensed telecom partners, and trusted infrastructure providers strictly to the extent required to execute message deliverability and voice call termination.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls—including TLS/SSL encryption for web and API endpoints, IP whitelist controls, firewalls, and restricted administrative access—to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of data.

Traffic logs and transmission metadata are retained only for the duration necessary for billing reconciliation, fraud prevention, and compliance with statutory retention periods.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses essential functional cookies to remember your theme preferences (such as Light/Dark mode) and maintain secure session states. You can control cookie preferences directly through your web browser settings; however, disabling essential cookies may affect site navigation.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact data stored with ButterflyIPRN. If you wish to exercise your data protection rights or withdraw consent for marketing communications, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

7. International Data Transfers

As a global telecommunications provider operating across international corridors, your data may be processed on secure servers located outside your jurisdiction. We ensure appropriate safeguards—including standard contractual clauses—are implemented for all cross-border data transfers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
